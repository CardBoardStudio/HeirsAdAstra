New in this playtest 33.38 Split Moves: leave some units behind when you move View changes
  • Split a group when you move: select a hex with a Move order and every combat ship there is ticked. Untick Settlers or Fleets, or lower a count, to leave them behind. The chosen units move at their slowest unit’s speed, so a Fleet that leaves its Settler moves 2. Each Move still uses one Move card. Explorers can split from each other but never travel with combat ships. Movement.
  • A brand-new market: every earlier advanced card is replaced by 36 new designs (26 normal, 10 military). Each costs more of at least one resource than your starting limit of 4, so raise your limits before buying. Move and Construct are no longer for sale. Card reference.
  • Ten face-up cards, one mixed supply: normal and military cards share the market. A bought military card joins your military deck instead of your discard pile. Buy.
  • Your military deck fights with you: every battle, your tactic hand is the two Fire and two Screen tactics plus each military card you own, once per battle. Combat.
  • Choose the Prestige target: pick 20 to 60 when you create the game; this experiment defaults to 40. Wonders and the Ascendancy Codex add permanent card Prestige. Victory.
  • Play with friends: host a table, share its invite link, and play in separate browsers; rivals fill the empty seats. Shared tables use these Capacity Cards rules and the table’s Prestige target. Undo works during your own turn, back to the last revealed card. Playing with friends.
  • Probe becomes Excavate: an Explorer now brings only a Probe card. When that Probe locates a relic, it is replaced by an Excavate card in your discard. Orders.
  • Tell us what you liked: the card gallery lists every card with its id (N01–N26, M01–M10) and lets you mark likes, dislikes and notes.

      QUICK REFERENCE

      How to command your faction

      Read the canonical rulebook · 33.38 ↗

      Your turn

      Convert cards for resources, then use one order, offer a trade, or pass. Passing immediately starts your Buy turn. Finish Buy, then the remaining factions continue Operations.

      Your round

      Produce → draw five → Operations (pass → your Buy turn) → everyone finishes → cleanup → victory check → next round.

      Your deck

      Normally buy one card. Upgrade original starters for two of one chosen resource type each. Trash eligible discarded cards, including starters, for three of one chosen resource type each. There is no one-card trash limit. Gained cards go to discard. Played and converted cards stay in play until shared cleanup. Successful Colonize, Probe, and Excavate orders are permanently trashed. Stored resources carry forward.

      Your goal

      Reach the table’s Prestige target (40 unless chosen otherwise when the game was created) at the end of a round. Colonies, excavated relics, and decisive combat victories contribute. Click a point total to see its breakdown. The rulebook includes costs, scoring, and current card clarifications.
