The waiting screen, the screen a link opens, the phone pairing panels and the public board now use the table's green felt, cream cards and gold, like the rest of the site, instead of navy and cyan. The public board's map shows the game's own tile and seat colours. Nothing about joining or starting changes. [Playing with others](rulebook.html#phone-pairing).
