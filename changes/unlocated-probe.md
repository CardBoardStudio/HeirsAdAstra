title: New relic signals and unlimited purchases
---
Probe skips relics you have already discovered and locates your Explorer’s nearest remaining unknown site. If you know every remaining relic, it warns you and keeps your card and action. [Probe](rulebook.html#orders).

Buy any number of affordable market cards during your Buy turn, including cards revealed by refills. Procurement Hub keeps its next-purchase discount. [Buying cards](rulebook.html#buy).
