title: A one-line, symbol-led Sector Scan
---
The Sector Scan's title line now holds the hex's resource, its name and symbols for its owner, building, colony site, relic and each faction's ships; point at a symbol to read it, and an X closes the scan. The Move shortcut names what it moves: Move Settlers, Move Fleets, Move Settlers & Fleets or Move Explorers. [Orders](rulebook.html#orders).
