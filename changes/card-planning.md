title: More selective card buying and useful exchange timing
---
Rivals account for printed construction and Colony benefits, capacity pressure, later purchases that can use a coupon, and the wait to draw a newly bought card. They enable a useful 2:1 trade before paying 4:1 and skip exchange orders that have no funded spending deficit. No card price, effect, or legal action changes. See [rival card timing](rulebook.html#rival-card-timing) and [card choices](rulebook.html#rival-cards).
