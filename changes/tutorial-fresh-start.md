compat: safe
title: Learn to play starts a new practice game unless you left one part-way
---
Choosing Learn to play when you had not completed its first step, or after finishing it, now deals a new practice game at Lesson 1 instead of reopening the earlier practice table. A walkthrough left part-way still resumes where you left it (Continue Learn to play), and a walkthrough restarted in one tab is no longer overwritten by another open tab. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play).
