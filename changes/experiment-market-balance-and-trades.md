title: Exchange cards, market prices and goal-directed rivals
---
Three cheap exchange cards let you choose a resource to trade at 2:1 until your Buy ends; eight market cards cost less and World Engine draws after its free build. New games default to 30 Prestige, and rivals improve production, purchase planning and goal-directed exchanges.
