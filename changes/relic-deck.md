title: Discover sixteen powerful alien relics
---
New games use sixteen relics with reinforcement, ship control, battle tricks, remote Colonies, market searches and capacity redistribution. Folded Warehouse and Omniscient Chorus keep their abilities. Archive Purge and Archive of Tides are retired; ordinary paid trashing remains. Existing games keep their dealt rules.
