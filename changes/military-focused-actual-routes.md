title: Military Focused checks small raid routes

Military Focused sends an early four-Fleet group into combat only when its actual legal route reaches the enemy in this Move. A blocked direct route that needs a longer detour makes the small group wait or regroup. Its two-world combat strategy and five-to-ten-Fleet groups remain.
