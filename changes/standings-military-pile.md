title: Standings table and a Military pile replace the Fleet panel
---
The Prestige race now doubles as a standings table: each faction's lane starts with its name and carries its Settlers, Explorers, Fleets, strongest fleet power, Colonies, Harvesters, Shipyards, excavated relics and cards bought, under one row of icon headings. Your military deck is a pile of its own beside In play; select it to see your tactics. The Fleet panel, which could not be closed, is gone, and a price you hold too little of shows as a faded chip instead of a dashed outline. [Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board), [Military deck](rulebook.html#military-deck).
