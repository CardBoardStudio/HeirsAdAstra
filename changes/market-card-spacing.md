title: Whole-card market reads cleanly on your Buy turn
---
On your Buy turn the whole cards no longer leave an empty strip beside them: the sheet is just wide enough for the cards, and the map shows beside it. Each card reads from its corners, symbols upper left and price upper right, and names wrap only between words. The Upgrades' effects are whole instead of cut off at the edge.
