title: Military Focused hunts in five-Fleet groups
---
The optional Military Focused rival settles one additional world, builds its forward Shipyard there, and hunts weaker enemy groups with five Fleets at a time. The original Military rival stays available; Harvesters, Capacity Modules, card draw, exchange cards and tactics support the new plan. [Computer rivals](rulebook.html#military-focused-rival).
