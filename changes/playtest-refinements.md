Resource limits stop at 15, Omniscient Chorus trashes after use, Gravity Scepter can summon onto your ships, and rivals pressure the Prestige leader. Relics use map targets and inline battle choices; clearer prices, upgrades, scoring, reference toggles and counting guides improve both tables.
