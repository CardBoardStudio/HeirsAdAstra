---
compat: breaking
title: Capacity is now a card
---
**Add 4 capacity** is now the **Capacity Module**, a card you buy from the Upgrades tray during Buy for 1 + the cards in your Storage pile, of each resource (the table shares 20). It enters your discard like any purchase; play it during Operations as your action to add 4 capacity points where you choose, and it goes to your permanent Storage pile beside your mat. You can no longer buy capacity off turn. [Resources & storage](rulebook.html#storage-allocation).
