title: Start a game alone or with others
---
The home page now offers two choices. **Play alone** asks only for the Prestige target and starts your game against three computer rivals. **Play with others** opens a waiting screen with the link ready to share, copy or scan; whoever joins says whether their device is their hand or shows the whole game, and you make the same choice for yours. Games already started open as before. [Victory target](rulebook.html#victory-target) · [Playing with others](rulebook.html#phone-pairing).
