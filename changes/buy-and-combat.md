title: Fleet protection and Buy exchanges
---
Fleets take combat hits before Settlers, followed by Explorers and Shipyards. Play held 2:1 exchange cards during your own Buy turn as well as Operations, and keep buying afterward. The Trash pile shows its price: 3 of one resource per card. [Combat](rulebook.html#combat-tactics) · [Exchange cards](rulebook.html#exchange-orders) · [Buy](rulebook.html#buy).
