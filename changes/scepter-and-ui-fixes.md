title: Choose Gravity Scepter ships and skip forced fight prompts
---
Gravity Scepter starts by highlighting enemy ships; choose their destination and how many of each type to move, leaving the rest behind. Battles skip “Stay and fight” when no paid Explorer escape is available. Mixed ship-count badges stay inside their hex. Max’s UI restores its earlier four resource symbols.
