Cheap now contains only the three draw-two and three 2:1 exchange designs. Ion Thrusters, Slipstream Charts, Rail Battery and Deflector Grid move to Medium. Six other former Cheap designs leave the new-game supply. Prices and effects stay the same. [Market rows](rulebook.html#market-tiers).
