title: Test stronger production planning and a more accessible market
---
Rivals plan additional Harvesters and compare market purchases with starter upgrades before spending their Buy budget. Eight movement, building, Colony and Buy cards have lower experimental prices; World Engine also draws one card after its successful free build. Monument of Accord is currently tested at 2 Matter, 6 Biomass and 2 Data, keeping its total price and 3-Prestige reward.
