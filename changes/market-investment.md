Rivals plan additional Harvesters and compare market purchases with starter upgrades before spending their Buy budget. Eight movement, building, Colony and Buy cards have lower experimental prices; World Engine costs 4 Matter, 1 Energy and 1 Biomass, fits the starting limits in the Medium row, and draws one card after its successful free build.
