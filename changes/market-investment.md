Rivals plan additional Harvesters and compare market purchases with starter upgrades before spending their Buy budget. Survey Network is currently tested at 2 Biomass and 1 Energy, keeping its total price and draw-two effect. [Rival planning](rulebook.html#rival-planning), [market cards](rulebook.html#cards).
