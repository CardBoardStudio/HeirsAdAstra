title: More room at Max’s table
---
Star background is now **Max’s UI**, with slate panels, soft blue accents, and more space for the map and your hand. Open **Relics**, **Market**, or **Basics** from three buttons on the left; Buy and excavation open the right bank automatically, and existing games keep their cards and rules.
