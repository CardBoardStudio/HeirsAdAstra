title: Phones are played sideways
---
On a phone, hold it sideways to play: the map fills the screen, your hand lies along the bottom as a fan with the turn's buttons at its right end, and the market, your mat, the comms log and the score track open from handles on the left edge. With the market closed the map has about two thirds of the screen, where it had about a third. Held upright, the game shows a prompt to turn the phone and waits until you do, without losing your place. A guidance or Learn to play note has one place at the right edge and never covers the map; a step about your mat or the score track opens that drawer. The rulebook, home page and waiting room still work held upright. [Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board).
