title: Learn to play says what is true and what is lit
---
The score track now teaches the real way to win: when a round ends with anyone at the target, the highest Prestige wins. Each note names the card, hex or button that is lit, and the battle note uses the tactics' card names. Rules, costs and saved games are unchanged. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play), [How to win](rulebook.html#victory), [Tactics](rulebook.html#combat-tactics).
