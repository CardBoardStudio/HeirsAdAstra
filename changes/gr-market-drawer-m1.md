title: The market opens from the table's edge
---
The supply (relics, market and Upgrades) is now a drawer on the table's right edge, closed until you open it with the **Market** tab, so the map has its room. Your Buy turn brings the market forward as whole cards with their pictures, and afterwards it goes back to how you had it; on a phone the Supply drawer does the same. [Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board).
