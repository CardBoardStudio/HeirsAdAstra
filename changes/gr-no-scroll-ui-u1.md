On a desktop window the game no longer scrolls: the whole window is one table, scaled as a whole to fit. The board lies in the middle with one lane per faction for the race to the Prestige goal along its edge; standings, a comms sheet and an open bank tray lie to its left; the turn and round markers and the whole supply at once (an Upgrades tray, the market and the alien relics) lie to its right; and your seat runs along the bottom: Deck and In play, your hand, a player mat with stock bowls, production and capacity tracks, and Discard with the turn's buttons ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). Cards look like printed card stock in a slight fan; point at one to read it whole, drag it onto a lit hex to play it or onto your mat to convert it. Market prices sit in each card's corner with a padlock on any resource above your limit, cards you can't buy yet are shaded with the reason ([Buy](rulebook.html#buy)), and held planets are ringed and buildings drawn in their owner's colour ([Reading ownership](rulebook.html#ownership-colours)). The page header folds into a menu and Trade opens in its own window.
