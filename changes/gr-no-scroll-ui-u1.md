title: A real table that fits your window
pr: 53

On a desktop window the game no longer scrolls: the whole window is one table, scaled as a whole to fit. The map is as large as the window allows. By its top-left corner a small printed track races every faction to the Prestige goal (the acting faction's marker glows; point at a marker for that faction's overview, select it for the breakdown), with a small comms card below it that opens into the whole log. The turn and round and the whole supply at once (an Upgrades tray, the market and the alien relics) lie to the right, and your seat runs along the bottom: Deck and In play, your hand, a player mat of pips (no numbers) for production, stock and a gold capacity fence, and Discard with the turn's buttons. Drag 4 of one resource onto another on your mat to trade with the bank ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). Cards look like printed card stock in a slight fan; point at one to read it whole, drag it onto a lit hex to play it or onto your mat to convert it. Market prices sit in each card's corner with a padlock on any resource above your limit, cards you can't buy yet are shaded with the reason ([Buy](rulebook.html#buy)), and held planets are ringed and buildings drawn in their owner's colour ([Reading ownership](rulebook.html#ownership-colours)). iPads get the same table, and phones a full-screen map with the hand, supply, mat, comms and track in drawers.
