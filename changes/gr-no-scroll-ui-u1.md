title: A real table that fits your window
pr: 53

On a desktop window the game no longer scrolls and is laid out like a table: the board in the middle with one lane per faction for the race to the Prestige goal along its edge, standings and an open bank tray on its surface, a compact visual comms feed top-right, then the turn prompt, phases and the whole supply at once (an Upgrades tray, the market and the alien relics), and your seat in front: your hand, Deck, In play and Discard as card piles, your reserves and the turn's buttons ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). Cards look like printed card stock in a slight fan; point at one to read it whole, drag it onto a lit hex to play it or onto your reserves to convert it. Market prices sit in each card's corner with a padlock on any resource above your limit, cards you can't buy yet are shaded with the reason ([Buy](rulebook.html#buy)), and held planets and buildings show their owner's colour ([Reading ownership](rulebook.html#ownership-colours)). The page header folds into a menu and Trade opens in its own window.
