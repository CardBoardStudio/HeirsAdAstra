title: A shorter rulebook with pictures
---
The rulebook is rewritten to be shorter and easier to follow. Each chapter opens with the one thing to know, exceptions and examples are marked, and diagrams show the round, a card's corners, your mat, the galaxy, the corner portal, a world, a battle and the market. Notes about the screen moved to an appendix, and the rulebook now scrolls on phones. The game itself is unchanged. [Start here](rulebook.html#start).
