title: Market-style relic choices and individual battle ships in Max’s UI
---
In **Max’s UI**, Oracle of the Unmade shows every market design in **Expensive, Moderate and Cheap** rows, with the Market’s price and capacity markers. Browse with the arrows or swipe, then confirm an available purchase. Keepers’ Archive uses the same card layout with discounted payment choices, and Echoes of the First Dawn lets you select up to two cards In play. Battles show each ship separately with its own remaining hull, so five Fleets appear as five Fleet icons.
