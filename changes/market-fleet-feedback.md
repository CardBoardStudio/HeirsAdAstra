title: Five-Fleet groups and clearer card references
---
Keep at most five Fleets per faction on a hex, including builds, retreats and relic effects; existing games keep their dealt rules. Select rival names to inspect their profiles, open the gallery from Market or Relics in your current interface, and use the earlier symbols throughout Max’s UI. Oracle hides designs with no copy in a remaining market deck.
