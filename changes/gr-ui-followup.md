The round's phases, the turn prompt and Fleet/Trade now sit at the bottom of the supply, next to your hand and the turn's buttons, and the supply starts at the top. On your turn your hand glows and gently breathes instead of a "Your turn" banner ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). Market cards show their whole name and as much of their effect as fits, so they read without pointing at them. A short key of the terrain colours and pieces lies in the map's empty corner.
