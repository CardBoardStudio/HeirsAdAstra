title: The turn lies with your seat

The table is rearranged around your seat ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). The left column holds, top to bottom, the Prestige track, the round's phases, the comms log, your fleets at a glance, the Upgrades (Move II, Construct II, Add 4 capacity, Move III, Construct III and the Trash pile, each a card that lights the resources you can pay with now) and the map key with each piece's price. The map fills the middle. The right column shows the relics and the market as real cards (63:88), with the market in three rows of three from its most expensive to its cheapest cards. Along the bottom are the menu and piles, your hand as a low fanned hand, the turn prompt with small turn buttons and Trade, a large player mat and Discard. On your turn your hand glows and gently breathes instead of a "Your turn" banner. Cards read like printed cards: the name and effect start at the top-left, the cost runs down the right edge, and effects use a shared set of icons (★ Prestige, Draw, Move, Hit, Miss, resource limit, Colonize, Build), which are listed in the map key and also appear on the starter cards. Resource icons are filled. The mat no longer offers a Discard; resources above a limit are still discarded automatically ([Resources](rulebook.html#resources)). Preview all relics is in the menu. Browser zoom works: zooming in makes the table's pieces larger and leaves the map less room.
