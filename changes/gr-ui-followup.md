title: The turn lies with your seat
pr: 74
---
The round's phases, the turn prompt and Fleet/Trade now sit at the bottom of the supply, next to your hand and the turn's buttons, and the supply starts at the top. On your turn your hand glows and gently breathes instead of a "Your turn" banner ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). Market cards show their whole name and as much of their effect as fits, so they read without pointing at them. A short key of the terrain colours and pieces lies in the map's empty corner. Your hand, the market, your mat and the map read first; the relics, upgrades, phases and comms sit back until you point at them. The mat no longer offers a Discard: resources above a limit are still discarded automatically ([Resources](rulebook.html#resources)). The +4 capacity token always shows its current price.
