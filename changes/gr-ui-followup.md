title: The turn lies with your seat
pr: 74
---
The round's phases, the turn prompt and Fleet/Trade now sit at the bottom of the supply, next to your hand and the turn's buttons, and the supply starts at the top. On your turn your hand glows and gently breathes instead of a "Your turn" banner ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). Market cards show their whole name and as much of their effect as fits, so they read without pointing at them; on wide windows market cards are real card shapes, as large as the space allows. A short key of the terrain colours and pieces lies in the map's empty corner. Your hand, the market, your mat and the map read first; the relics, upgrades, phases and comms sit back until you point at them. The mat no longer offers a Discard: resources above a limit are still discarded automatically ([Resources](rulebook.html#resources)). An Upgrades row directly above your mat holds Move II, Move III, Construct II, Construct III and Add 4 capacity as cards, each lighting the resources you can pay with now. The supply lies in rows: the relics on top, then the market from its most expensive cards down to its cheapest. Your hand is a fanned hand held low, each card showing a narrow strip with its name; point at a card to lift it whole. The market fills the supply's full width. The comms log and your fleets at a glance sit at the top-left, the map key with each piece's price at the bottom-left, and the round's phases at the top-right; Preview all relics is in the menu. The market shows three rows of three, the upgrades and the Trash pile lie in a 3x2 grid above your mat, and the turn's buttons and Trade sit right of the mat above Discard. Supply cards read like printed cards, with their cost down the right edge and their effects in a shared set of icons (★ Prestige, Draw, Hit, Miss, Colonize, Build) listed in the map key; resource icons are filled.
