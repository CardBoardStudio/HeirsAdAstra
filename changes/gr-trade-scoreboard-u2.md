title: Trade pulls out from the left edge; names sit beside the standings
---
Trade is now a tab on the table's left edge. Select it to pull the trade channel out beside the map; the tab, Close or Escape tucks it back, and the tab still counts the offers waiting for you. In the standings, each faction's name now sits directly beside its counts, with the race to the Prestige goal after them. [Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board).
