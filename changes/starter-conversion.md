title: Level I starter conversion and resource-card removals
---
Only level I Move and Construct starters convert to resources; upgraded starters, unit cards, market cards, and relics cannot. Level III draws only when its order is used. [Resources](rulebook.html#resources), [orders](rulebook.html#orders).

Remove cards whose played effects grant resources, plus Galactic Senate Seat. Monument of Accord and Dyson Lattice still award points when bought, then circulate as inert cards until trashed. [Cards](rulebook.html#cards).
