compat: safe
title: Prismatic Reservoir uses the capacity selector in Max’s UI
---
Play **Prismatic Reservoir** in Max’s UI to adjust four resource counters, just like a Capacity Module. Its counters are signed changes: increases and decreases must total **zero** before confirming. Each row previews the new limit, with the existing minimum of 4 and held-resource protection. [Prismatic Reservoir](rulebook.html#relic-capacity).
