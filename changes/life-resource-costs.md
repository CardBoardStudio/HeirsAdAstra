title: Life and distinct resource costs
---
Biomass is now Life. Settlers cost 1 Matter and 2 Life; Fleets cost 2 Matter and 2 Energy; Shipyards cost 2 Matter, 2 Energy and 1 Data. Found a Colony for 3 Life, or excavate for 1 Energy and 3 Data. Captures still cost 1 Life, and printed free or reduced Colony costs still apply. Start a new game for these costs. [Resource costs](rulebook.html#resource-costs).
