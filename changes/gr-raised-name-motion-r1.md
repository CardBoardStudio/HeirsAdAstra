compat: safe
title: A raised card's name never runs under a corner
---
A hand card you point at or tap shows its whole name, between its corners or just under them, also when your device is set to reduce motion and when you raise the card with a tap on a tablet. Before, a name such as Construct could run under the right-hand corner. [Read a card from its corners](rulebook.html#card-corners).
