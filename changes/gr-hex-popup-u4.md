compat: safe
title: The map shows what you can play on a hex
---
Point at a hex (or focus it with Tab) and the cards in your hand that could start an order there rise and glow. Select the hex to open its Sector Scan as a note beside it, its arrow aimed at the hex. It lists those orders with each card's picture; choosing one starts the order as Use order or a drag would. Escape or a click elsewhere closes it. [Your basic orders](rulebook.html#orders).
