title: Learn to play starts with one glowing card
---
Begin with the marked Construct and its first build. You already have the Matter and Data: convert a Move for the missing Energy, then use the Construct to build a Harvester. Only the suggested card glows and gently grows. Its instructions appear when you hover, focus or touch it, and stay available while you complete that step. The table and mat introductions follow your first build. Rules, costs and ordinary saved games are unchanged. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play), [Resources](rulebook.html#resources), [Building](rulebook.html#building).
