title: The market splits into three rows by price
---
The market is now three decks sorted by total price: **Expensive** (8 or more), **Medium** (6–7) and **Cheap** (5 or less), each with three face-up cards below the relics. A bought card is replaced from its own row's deck; when that deck runs out, the slot stays empty. [Market rows](rulebook.html#market-tiers).
