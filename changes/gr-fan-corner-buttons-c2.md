title: Card corners look like buttons
---
Every card in your hand shows its two corner buttons as tabs set into the card's corners, resting in the fan or raised: the order corner tinted, the convert corner in its resource's colour, each with a raised edge that flattens when pressed. When a corner's action is not open (a rival's turn, another phase) its tab is dashed and dimmed but still shows what the card does. On a touch screen the first tap on a resting card raises it and the next press acts. [Read a card from its corners](rulebook.html#card-corners), [Orders](rulebook.html#orders).
