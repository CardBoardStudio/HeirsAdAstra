title: Bigger card corners that look like buttons
---
Every card in your hand shows its two corner buttons as big tabs set into the card's corners, about a third of the card's width, resting in the fan or raised. The order corner is tinted, the convert corner is in its resource's colour, and each has a raised edge that flattens when pressed. When a corner's action is not open (a rival's turn, another phase) its tab is dashed and dimmed but still shows what the card does. A name that no longer fits between a raised card's corners sits under them, and a resting card's name wraps onto two lines or gets a little smaller instead of being cut off. On a touch screen the first tap on a resting card raises it and the next press acts. [Read a card from its corners](rulebook.html#card-corners), [Orders](rulebook.html#orders).
