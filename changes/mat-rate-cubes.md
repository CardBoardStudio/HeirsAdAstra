title: Rate is shown with cubes on the player mat
---
On the green table's player mat, each resource's Rate pieces are now small cubes in that resource's colour, like the wooden cubes of a board game, and the legend's Rate swatch is a cube too. Now and Capacity stay round, and the empty slots, values and trades are unchanged. [Resources & storage](rulebook.html#resources), [Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat).
