compat: safe
title: The player mat shows one piece per value
---
On the green table's player mat, each resource row now has one round Now piece on the slot for your stock and one Rate cube, in that resource's colour, on the slot for your production, like markers on a track. The first slot is 0, and slots 5, 10, 15 and 20 have a brighter rim. When your stock equals your limit, the Now piece stands on the gold capacity marker. The legend's Rate swatch is a cube too. Values, trades and limits are unchanged. [Resources & storage](rulebook.html#resources), [Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat).
