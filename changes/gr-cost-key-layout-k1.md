title: A larger map key with prices beside their names
---
The map key's names and card icons are larger, each price sits just after its name, faint lines part the rows, and each resource's symbols form one cluster: two side by side, three as a small triangle. On an upright tablet the costs are in the Map key, not on the table. [Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board).
