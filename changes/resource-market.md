Cheap now has five Draw 2 designs and three 2:1 designs, with two copies each. Logistics Briefing costs 2 Matter and 1 Energy; Living Archives costs 2 Life and 1 Matter. Cheap prices depend less on Data, and Medium’s Flak Screen and Cargo Expansion each cost 5 Life and 1 Matter. Start a new game; existing tables keep their dealt prices.
