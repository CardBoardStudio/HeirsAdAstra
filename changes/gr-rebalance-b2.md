**Toned down:** Ascendancy Codex gives 1 Prestige per use without drawing, Dyson Lattice grants 4 Prestige, and Hyperspace Vaults raises each limit by 1 per use. Folded Warehouse raises each limit by 2 (up to 15), draws two cards, then trashes itself; its excavation Prestige stays. Genesis Charter costs 2 Matter, 7 Life and 1 Data. **New tactic:** Torpedo Barrage (M11) gives +3 hits and prevents 3 hits, for 6 Energy, 2 Life and 2 Data. Later market retirements and cheaper prices stay in place. [Card reference](rulebook.html#cards).
