compat: safe
title: A bigger mat, buttons where you use them, cards with corner boxes
---
Your player mat is larger, with bigger pips and icons. **Convert level I starters** sits left of the mat and appears only while you hold a level I starter you may convert; **Trade** sits beside it; a smaller **Pass & buy** or **Finish Buy** stays just above Discard ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)).
