title: The whole supply on the right, a bigger map, turn buttons over Discard
---
The Upgrades (Move II, Construct II, Add 4 capacity, then Move III, Construct III and the Trash pile) rejoin the supply on the right, under the market, so the right column is six rows of three cards: relics, the three market rows and the two upgrade rows ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). All supply cards are one size, smaller than before but wide enough to read whole, and take real card proportions on tall windows. The left-hand panels are narrower and lie in the map's empty corners, so the map is bigger. **Pass & buy** or **Finish Buy**, **Convert level I starters** and **Trade** now stack just above Discard, right of your mat ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)); the written turn prompt is gone, since your glowing hand already shows your turn.
