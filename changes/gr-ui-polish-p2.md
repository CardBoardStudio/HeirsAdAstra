compat: safe
title: A bigger map and mat, buttons where you use them, cards with corner boxes
---
The map is as large as your window allows: it reaches between the narrow left-hand panels and down between your hand, piles and mat, the map key sits in the middle of the left column, and on a wide window the supply cards grow wider instead of leaving bare table ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). Your player mat is larger, with bigger pips and icons. **Convert level I starters** sits left of the mat and appears only while you hold a level I starter you may convert; **Trade** sits beside it; a smaller **Pass & buy** or **Finish Buy** stays just above Discard ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). A card's cost is one box in its top-right corner, and what a level I starter converts into is one box in its top-left corner; cards that cannot convert have no such box, so upgrade cards no longer say so. Upgrade cards use the card icons, and Construct III's upgrade card prints what Construct III does ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)). Pointing at a card in your hand raises that card itself into the whole card above your hand, instead of showing a copy beside the map ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). Choosing **Use order** lights the same hexes as dragging the card; choose a lit hex to play it there.
