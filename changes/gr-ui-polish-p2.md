---
compat: safe
title: A bigger mat, buttons where you use them, cards with corner boxes
---
Your player mat is larger, with bigger pips and icons. **Convert level I starters** sits left of the mat and appears only while you hold a level I starter you may convert; **Trade** sits beside it; a smaller **Pass & buy** or **Finish Buy** stays just above Discard ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). A card's cost is one box in its top-right corner, and what a level I starter converts into is one box in its top-left corner; cards that cannot convert have no such box, so upgrade cards no longer say so. Upgrade cards use the card icons, and Construct III's upgrade card prints what Construct III does ([Shared board](rulebook.html#shared-board)).
