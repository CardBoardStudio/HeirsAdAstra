compat: safe
title: Learn to play follows the round as you play it
---
Converting the rest of your hand now lights one card's convert corner at a time, and its ghost goes to that card's own row on your mat. When your hand runs out, the walkthrough says the table passed you to Buy and lights that notice, and its step count no longer skips a step you never see. Relics now start with only your Probe / Excavate card and a Move in hand, so reaching the relic ends the round and you excavate in the next one, without throwing cards away. Rules, costs and ordinary saved games are unchanged. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play).
