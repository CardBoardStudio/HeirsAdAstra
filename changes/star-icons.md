title: Symbols drawn as constellations
---
Every symbol in the game is redrawn as a small constellation of four-point stars: the four resources, the card icons (Move, Build, Colonize, Draw, Prestige, Hit, Shield, Limit, Trade), the units and buildings, and Excavate's relic diamond. Small symbols use solid lines and fuller stars so each one stays distinct at 16 px. The card gallery lists all twenty by name. [Card corners](rulebook.html#card-corners).
