title: Learn to play, and a guide on the table
---
New players can choose **Learn to play** on the home page or in the game menu: a practice game teaches every part of a turn on the real table, one step at a time, and waits for you to do each one. In your own games, a short note suggests a useful next step each turn and explains a rule the first time it matters; switch it off with Guidance in the menu. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play) · [Guidance](rulebook.html#guidance).
