compat: breaking
title: Choose a resource for better bank exchanges
---
Add three inexpensive exchange cards. Choose a resource to give at 2:1 until your Buy turn ends. Normal games keep their tiered market; a headless research configuration can buy any remaining design at its normal cost to compare cards with four Market rivals. [Exchange orders](rulebook.html#exchange-orders), [simulation market](rulebook.html#simulation-catalog-market).

New solo games and shared waiting rooms default to 30 Prestige. Existing saved goals stay fixed. [Victory target](rulebook.html#victory-target).
