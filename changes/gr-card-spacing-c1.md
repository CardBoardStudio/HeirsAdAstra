compat: safe
title: Read a card from its corners
---
Every card shows what it does as symbols in its upper-left corner (one hammer on Construct, two on Construct II) and, in the upper-right corner, what a level I starter converts to or a market card's price. In your hand those corners are the card's buttons: press the symbols to use the order, the resource to convert. Your hand is a fan in which both corners of every card show; point at a card to raise it whole. [Read a card from its corners](rulebook.html#card-corners).
