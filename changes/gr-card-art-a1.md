title: Card art from the National Gallery of Art
---
Every card now shows a public-domain painting, print or photograph from the National Gallery of Art, Washington: Romantic seascapes and landscapes for market and starter cards, naval battles for the military deck, and Piranesi engravings and star charts for the alien relics. On the table a market card's picture fills the space its text leaves. The card gallery lists every work and its artist. The art never changes what a card does ([Card reference](rulebook.html#card-art)). The game menu, rulebook and card gallery end with the version and a short art notice.
