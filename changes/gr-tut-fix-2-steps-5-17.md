title: Learn to play keeps the hex you select and flies the right ship
---
In Learn to play, the hex you select to look at your home world keeps its Sector Scan open. In the Explorer's move, your Settler first steps aside so the Move you drop flies the Explorer, and the walkthrough points only at the Explorer's destinations. Rules, costs and ordinary saved games are unchanged. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play), [Movement](rulebook.html#movement).
