---
compat: safe
title: Cards you can almost pick up
---
Cards look like printed card stock and lean toward your pointer, and your hand is held in a slight fan. Point at a hand card to see it whole beside the map, or drag it onto a lit hex to play it ([Player mat](rulebook.html#player-mat)). Draws, plays, conversions, purchases and production now move between your piles, the market and your reserves. Market prices sit in each card's corner with a padlock on any resource above your limit ([Buy](rulebook.html#buy)), and your reserves show stock as pips out of each limit.
