title: Learn to play lights the card you use next
---
Learn to play no longer has a "Find a Move" button: your first step lights the Move card to convert, and a step that waits for you has no button that does it for you. Doing it on the table moves you on. On a narrow screen the hand scrolls to the lit card as each step starts. Rules, costs and ordinary saved games are unchanged. [Learn to play](rulebook.html#learn-to-play).
